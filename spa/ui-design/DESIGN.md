## Brand & Style

### Personality & Academic Narrative
The design system embodies the rigor of classic critical monographs combined with modern computational linguistics and corpus analysis. It positions itself at the intersection of historical philology, quantitative text analysis, and archival stewardship. The atmosphere is intellectual, dispassionate, reverent, and analytically sharp. 

### Aesthetic Philosophy
The system combines an **Editorial Academic** layout with **Modern Scientific Instrument** utilities:
- **Warm Archival Grounding**: Background surfaces take inspiration from vellum, alabaster, and unbleached rag paper, eliminating stark digital glare for prolonged comparative reading.
- **Bilateral Corpus Distinction**: Biblical text structures and Quranic codices maintain distinct chromatic anchors (deep Lapis Indigo vs. Emerald Malachite) without destabilizing the neutral academic plane.
- **Typographic Precision**: A dialectic between literary, historical serifs for structural and sacred text display, razor-sharp geometric grotesques for analytical tooling and navigational schemas, and tabular monospaced letterforms for computational metrics, lemmata tokens, and concordance indexes.
- **Glass & Membrane Tiers**: Translucent frosted panels evoke physical microscope slides or archival overlay paper, providing contextual commentary without obscuring parallel textual columns.

## Colors

### Chromatic Architecture
The chromatic strategy utilizes a calibrated scholarly ink palette built upon warm parchment foundations, reserving chromatic vibrancy strictly for hermeneutic taxonomy, comparative alignment, and computational visualization.

- **Foundational Surfaces**:
  - `Canvas / Parchment Base`: `#F8F6F0` (warm alabaster, low eye-strain).
  - `Surface Elevated / Vellum`: `#FFFFFF` with 80% opacity and warm tinting.
  - `Surface Subdued / Pale Ochre`: `#EFECE4` (used for comparative margins and secondary panels).
- **Primary Ink & Neutrals**:
  - `Primary Ink`: `#1E293B` (slate iron-gall ink for primary narrative and metadata).
  - `Secondary Ink`: `#475569` (for apparatus criticus, chapter summaries, and cross-references).
  - `Muted / Structural Slate`: `#94A3B8` (for hairline borders, grid rules, and morphological brackets).
  - `Border Subtle`: `#E2DDD3` (archival rule width: 1px).
- **Corpus Semantics**:
  - **Biblical Corpus**: `#1D3557` (Deep Lapis) to `#2563EB` (Cobalt Accent). Used for text segmentation, syntactic parsing nodes, and textual variants in the Septuagint/Masoretic/Vulgate traditions.
  - **Quranic Corpus**: `#065F46` (Deep Emerald) to `#059669` (Jade Accent). Used for Surah verse tracking, root-and-pattern tagging (Jizr), and vocalization markers.
- **Philological Accents**:
  - `Gold / Bronze Leaf`: `#B45309` / `#D97706` (for semantic overlap, critical apparatus sigla, statistical significance highlights, and structural colophons).

## Typography

### Structural Role Assignment
- **Newsreader**: Assigned to high-level architectural headers, critical monograph entries, and the primary scriptural text view (`scripture-display`). It features optical sizing attributes that preserve graceful thin-stroke terminals and calligraphic serifs, reminiscent of early modern scholarly press prints.
- **Inter**: Assigned to structural labels, contextual annotations, analytical sidebars, search interfaces, and UI controls. Provides neutral, undistorted functional clarity alongside dense textual evidence.
- **JetBrains Mono**: Strictly applied to linguistic metadata: concordance frequencies, n-gram vector coordinates, TF-IDF weights, lemma tokens, verse addresses (e.g., `GEN.01:01`, `QUR.02:255`), and critical apparatus sigla.

### Academic Typesetting Standards
- Sacred and original language transcriptions must be set with a line height ratio between 1.6 and 1.7 to avoid collision with vocalization points (harakat), cantillation marks, or morphological gloss accents.
- Small caps and proportional oldstyle numerals must be prioritized across Newsreader body texts for dates, manuscript catalog numbers, and verse markers.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Philosophy: Comparative Parallel Collation
The layout is governed by a **bilateral scholarly grid** designed to render dual comparative streams without horizontal imbalance. The canvas is divided into a 12-column variable module system with specialized sub-grids for critical apparatus collation:

- **Desktop (>= 1280px)**: 12 columns with 2rem gutters. Layout allows for:
  - *Standard Dual Collation Mode*: Left 5 columns (Biblical / Septuagint / Vulgate corpus), Right 5 columns (Quranic / Classical Arabic corpus), flanking a persistent 2-column center alignment gutter reserved for semantic linkage lines, root-overlap markers, and Jaccard similarity indices.
  - *Apparatus Dock*: Persistent 3-column auxiliary drawer on the outer right edge for grammatical treebanks and vector scatterplots.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8 columns with 1.5rem gutters. The parallel columns transition into synchronized tab views or vertically split scroll panes with synchronized anchor jumps.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with 0.75rem gutters and 1rem canvas margins. Comparative parallel text reflows into an alternating sequential view, marked by top-anchored corpus color-pills (`Lapis` and `Emerald`).

### Spacing Cadence
Spacing follows an exact 4px/8px modular base rhythm. Margins between text verses and grammatical interlinear rows use `space-xs` and `space-sm` to maintain lexical binding. Analytical sections, monograph breaks, and visualization panels rely on `space-xl` and `space-2xl` to mimic academic book plates and folio margins.

## Elevation & Depth

### Philological Depth & Membrane Layering
Visual hierarchy avoids generic heavy drop shadows. Instead, it relies on paper-stack layering, translucent archival vellum, and micro-fine hairline delineation.

- **Base Canvas (Level 0)**: Uncoated alabaster parchment (`#F8F6F0`). Completely matte, textured neutral foundation.
- **Surface Membrane / Text Card (Level 1)**: White alabaster (`#FFFFFF`) with 1px border of `#E2DDD3`. Subtle shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.03), 0 4px 12px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.02)`.
- **Translucent Overlays & Morphological Tooltips (Level 2)**: Frosted parchment glassmorphism. Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px) saturate(140%)`. Border: `1px solid rgba(226, 221, 211, 0.8)`. Soft ambient elevation: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.04)`.
- **Floating Scientific Inspector Panels (Level 3)**: Fixed comparative inspectors and vector coordinate floating bars. Background: `rgba(248, 246, 240, 0.95)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`. Border: `1px solid rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.4)`. Shadow: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.1), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.04)`.
- **Corpus Anchoring Glow**: When an active token is focused, elevation is reinforced via an ultra-soft 4px diffuse aura using the respective corpus hue (`rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)` for Biblical; `#059669` at 15% opacity for Quranic).

## Shapes

### Restrained Architectural Geometry
The shape language reflects archival folios, academic indexes, and precision instruments:
- **Base Geometry (`roundedness: 1`)**: Standard buttons, input search fields, badge pills, and text cells feature micro-rounded corners (0.25rem / 4px). This preserves the clean, crisp silhouette of physical print matrices while avoiding the harshness of raw 90-degree corners.
- **Panels & Cards**: Larger content containers, interlinear comparative boxes, and visualization canvas borders utilize `rounded-lg` (0.5rem / 8px).
- **Inspection Badges & Metric Chips**: Retain structural rectangular forms with subtle 2px to 4px beveling, preventing modern UI pill-bloat from disrupting academic gravitas.

## Components

### 1. Interactive Text Tokens & Lemmatized Words
- **Visual Style**: Lemmatized tokens sit within reading columns with transparent background borders. On cursor hover or pointer focus, words transition into a soft archival highlight: `#F1EEDB` with an underline matching the corpus accent.
- **Micro-Interaction**: Clicking a token reveals a glassmorphic floating apparatus showing root morphology, Strong's/Buckwalter concordance, occurrence frequency, and cross-corpus vector proximity.

### 2. Comparative Side-by-Side Panels
- **Anatomy**: Dual matched scrolling viewports. 
- **Headers**: Sticky academic header bars displaying book/surah identifiers in `Newsreader` (Semibold), flanked by corpus color badges (`#1D3557` Biblical, `#065F46` Quranic).
- **Synchronized Alignment**: Interactive anchor lines drawn across the central gutter between verses with calculated semantic or thematic links, highlighted in soft Gold (`#D97706`).

### 3. Filter & Computational Control Bars
- **Anatomy**: Clean modular instrument bar positioned beneath global navigation.
- **Components**: Segmented controllers for analytical models (e.g., *Cosine Similarity*, *Cross-Entropy*, *Latent Dirichlet Allocation*). 
- **States**: Inactive states display as muted slate text on clear backgrounds. Active segments show a crisp alabaster card (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border of `#E2DDD3` and subtle elevation.

### 4. Metric Indicators & Morphological Chips
- **Anatomy**: Compact, high-density tags displaying linguistic metrics (`P-val: 0.002`, `N-gram: 3`, `Cosine: 0.842`).
- **Typography**: `JetBrains Mono` (`metric-mono`).
- **Surface**: Pale slate or warm cream tint with subtle borders (`#E2DDD3`). For corpus-specific metrics, uses 10% tinted fills of `#1D3557` or `#065F46` with solid primary ink labels.

### 5. Buttons & Action Utilities
- **Primary Buttons**: Deep navy slate ink (`#1E293B`) background, pristine parchment text (`#F8F6F0`), 4px border radius. Hover state: `#334155` with 150ms ease transition.
- **Corpus Direct Action Buttons**: Deep Lapis (`#1D3557`) for Biblical corpus actions; Deep Emerald (`#065F46`) for Quranic corpus queries.
- **Secondary / Ghost Buttons**: Archival parchment background (`#FFFFFF`), 1px structural slate border (`#CBD5E1`), text `#1E293B`.

### 6. Interactive Apparatus Tooltips
- **Backdrop**: Translucent alabaster glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)`) with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.
- **Layout**: Top header with root identification in original scripts, middle section showing English gloss and lexical parse trees, footer with statistical corpus distribution bar.

### 7. Form Inputs & Lexical Query Fields
- **Anatomy**: Inset parchment text input with subtle border (`#CBD5E1`).
- **Focus State**: Hairline border transition to Bronze (`#B45309`) with an ambient outer ring of `rgba(180, 83, 9, 0.12)`. Monospace query inputs automatically format computational operators (e.g., `AND`, `NEAR/5`, `LEMMA:`) into styled inline syntax tokens.
